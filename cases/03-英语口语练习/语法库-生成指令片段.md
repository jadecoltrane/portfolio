# 语法库 · 生成指令片段

直接贴给每日课的 AI。规则正文写英文,因为它要进的那份提示词是英文的;中文行只是给你看的说明。

---

## 一、给每一行加 `kind`

**判据一句话:另一个形式是不是错的?**

```
Every row in the usage store carries a `kind`. To decide which:

- The other form is WRONG                                  -> kind: rule
- The other form is correct but means something different  -> kind: choice
- The other form is correct and means the same, only more
  or less formal                                           -> kind: register
- There is no "other form" — she simply never reaches for
  this structure                                           -> kind: pattern

The four kinds fail in different ways, so they are tested in different ways.
Never test one kind with another kind's question.
```

---

## 二、四种各怎么存、怎么考

```
kind: choice   e.g. I'd / I'm going to / I'll
  Store   the contrast set; one line on how to pick; HER situation; one short
          example per member.
  Test    a situation card she answers in her own words; judge whether the
          form matches the intent, not whether she used a target expression.
  Review  +2 days  intent spelled out, form category named
          +10 days intent hidden in the situation, form not named
          +30 days no card — check her free output instead

kind: rule   only one correct form
  e.g.    third-person -s
          a countable noun needs a determiner
          suggest that he do
          no inversion in embedded questions: know what time it is
          NO on/in/at before this/that/next/last/every
            (on next Monday, in last week, on every morning are all wrong;
             "On that day, ..." is the one surviving marked form, used
             sentence-initially for a formal or narrative tone)
  Store   the rule in one line; two or three wrong examples, preferably ones
          she actually wrote; the exception if there is one.
  Test    a correction task.
          1st pass  name the rule: "the verb form in this sentence is off"
          2nd pass  don't name it: "there is one form error here, find it"
          3rd pass  no card — check her free output
  Note    Prefer code over AI for this kind. Third-person -s, a missing
          determiner, embedded-question inversion, and on|in|at followed by
          this/that/next/last/every can all be scanned directly.

kind: pattern   a structure she never reaches for
  e.g.    Having tested it with five users, we...
          It took us two sprints to...
          What surprised me was...
  Store   the structure; one model sentence; the flat sentence of hers that
          it rewrites.
  Test    NOT a review card. Issue it as a production requirement for the day:
          "In today's follow-up chat, start at least one sentence with
          Having + past participle."
          Used = passed. Used naturally two days running = mastered.
  Why     A structure tested as a card becomes a memorised template: she can
          fill the blank and still never reach for it while speaking.

kind: register   same meaning, different formality
  e.g.    won't / will not
          I hope it doesn't rain / I hope it won't rain
  Store   the pair; what each SOUNDS like (not when each is correct); her
          situation.
  Note    Her bias is one-directional — she drifts formal. Train downward
          only; she does not need practice sounding more formal.
  Test    give a casual scene and judge the register of what she produces.
  Cheaper No card at all — carry it as a standing check in the daily review
          of her writing (see below).
```

## 三、行从哪来:批改里加三条常驻检查

```
While correcting her writing each day, flag three things, not one:

1. a wrong form                      -> candidate `rule` row
2. correct but clumsy — meaning is
   fine, structure is primitive      -> candidate `pattern` row
3. correct but stiff — register too
   high for speech                   -> candidate `register` row

Only #1 exists today. #2 and #3 are the new ones, and they cost almost
nothing: the correction step is already reading every sentence she wrote.

`choice` rows keep coming from her own questions, as before.
```

---

## 四、上限和不收的东西

```
Cap: at most ONE usage card per day, shared across all four kinds, outside
the 20-card review limit. `pattern` production requirements do not count —
they ride on the follow-up chat. If it ever comes down to one more usage
card or the speaking drill, drop the card.

Do not store:
- a one-off misunderstanding that has been cleared up. Criterion: does it
  predict an error she will actually make? If not, leave it out — otherwise
  the store fills with questions already settled. (Exception: if she really
  does produce the error, it becomes a `rule` row.)
- anything that needs an essay to explain. That belongs in her notes, not
  in the lesson.
- pronunciation and spelling.
```

---

## 五、顺带一个指标

```
Count her contraction rate in free output — won't vs will not, I'm vs I am,
it's vs it is. It is a proxy for register, it is machine-countable, and it
is an outcome measure rather than a process one: it does not ask what she
remembers, only how she actually speaks when nobody is prompting her.
```
